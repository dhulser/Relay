It listens to your Mac, not your microphone

Whatever is coming out of your speakers is what Relay reads — a browser tab, a video call, a file on your desktop. Nothing is recorded, and the audio keeps playing normally while you read along.

Knows who is speaking

Tags each line with the voice that said it, so a conversation stays readable when people talk over each other.

Follows the language

Works out what is being spoken as it goes. If the room switches from Spanish to French, the subtitles keep up.

Stays where you put it

Drag the captions anywhere on screen. They never steal focus, and they sit above full-screen apps.

Nine languages

Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Japanese, Korean, Chinese and English, in any direction.

Setting it up

  1. 1

    Move Relay to Applications

    It lives in the menu bar. There is no window to keep track of.

  2. 2

    Paste in a key

    Relay uses your own Anthropic or OpenAI account for the translation, so you pay them directly and nothing goes through us. The key is kept in your Keychain.

  3. 3

    Press Start listening

    macOS will ask once for permission to hear your Mac. Then play something.

Your audio stays on your Mac

Speech is turned into text on your own machine — that part never touches a network. Only the words are sent off for translating, and only to the account you bring. No recordings, no transcripts kept, no account to make.
