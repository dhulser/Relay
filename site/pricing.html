Pricing

Relay is free. You pay your provider for what it uses.

Add your own Anthropic or OpenAI key and the bill goes straight to them, at their prices, for exactly the translation Relay asked for. There is no Relay account and nothing to subscribe to. Here is what that comes to.

An hour of talking

The figures below are for an hour of someone actually speaking, about twelve phrases a minute, at the providers' published prices. Local mode sends only text, so a quiet hour costs almost nothing. Instant mode bills every minute of audio, whether anyone is talking or not.

Local mode recognises speech on your Mac with Whisper, then translates the text. Instant mode streams the audio to OpenAI and shows words while the speaker is still talking, which is why it costs fifty times more.

Larger models

Relay defaults to the fastest, cheapest good model from each provider. If you would rather have more careful phrasing, pick a larger one in Settings. Each line waits a little longer and costs more.

Getting a key

Either provider will do; Relay works the same with both. Both let you prepay or set a monthly limit, so a session you forgot to stop cannot run up a bill.

Anthropic console.anthropic.com. Create a key under API Keys, paste it into Relay's Settings. OpenAI platform.openai.com. One key covers both Local and Instant mode.

Keys are kept in the macOS Keychain. Relay never sees them anywhere else, and neither do we.

Prefer not to manage a key? Relay Hosted.

Relay brings the keys. $2 a month covers the account, then you pay for the time Relay spends listening: 40¢ an hour in Local mode, $3.50 an hour in Instant mode, billed by the minute. Stop it and the meter stops. A month can never come to more than $50; Relay pauses at the cap and starts again on the 1st.

Local mode still recognises speech on your Mac and sends only text; Instant mode streams audio through Relay to OpenAI, the same as with your own key.
